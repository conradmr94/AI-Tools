The response's opening line or paragraph says the change is large (hundreds of changed lines) and offers to go through it commit by commit or in smaller pieces. After that, a short overview is allowed and expected: a brief "In short" summary and a list of the commits with a line or so each. What fails: walking through the code in detail (sections on specific files, functions or line ranges) instead of offering to, or mentioning the size only after such a walkthrough.
